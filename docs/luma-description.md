Join a Claude Community Event, hosted by Official Claude Ambassadors, for everyone who loves building with Claude!

Robotaxis fill the streets. AI agents read our inboxes. Robots are showing up on the sidewalk. Who is building the guardrails?

San Francisco is where AI meets everyday life first, and most people here never got a say in how it should behave. For one evening, we're putting builders on the problem.

This Claude Impact Lab is a one-hour micro-hack on trust and safety. Three short talks set the scene, then teams pick a track, point Claude at real public data, and build a working solution before the night is out. Then we demo.

**Tracks**

- Agents in Inboxes: stop a stranger's email from giving your AI assistant orders
- Spot the Rogue Agent: a team of agents finds and shuts out the imposter
- Robotaxis & Robots: an SF robotaxi report card from public crash data
- Privacy: what surveillance tech covers the Bay Area, and where the data goes
- Personal Safety: tell real from fake in the age of AI scams and deepfakes

Every track comes with curated public datasets and step-by-step instructions written for coding agents: https://github.com/sergeicu/trust-safety-impact-lab

**Agenda**

- 6:00 – 6:30 Registration and food
- 6:30 – 7:00 Three 10-minute talks
- 7:00 – 8:00 Hacking
- 8:15 – 9:00 Presentations
- 9:00 – 10:00 Overflow and social

**What to expect**

- Teams of founders, engineers, researchers, and designers, mixed on purpose
- Hands-on building with Claude, with people who ship with it daily in the room
- Live demos to close the night
- A path forward for the best ideas: promising teams can talk with [Antler](https://www.antler.co/?utm_source=luma) about its residency and investor network (no acceptance or investment is promised)

**Who should come**

Engineers and researchers who have seen how AI fails up close. Founders, and people quietly thinking about becoming one. Trust and safety, security, and policy people who want to build, not just critique.

No coding experience needed. Bring your ideas, and Claude helps you build. Bring a laptop.

**What you get**

- Claude API credits
- Official Anthropic swag
- Free food
